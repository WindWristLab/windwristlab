Questions, bug reports or feedback about WristWindLab? Write to the address below and mention your watch model, watchOS version and app version.

wristwindlab@proton.me

Quick answers

Is it an anemometer?
No. It is a research prototype that gives a relative estimate until you calibrate it against a real anemometer.
Why does it need the microphone?
To measure the low-frequency noise level that wind creates. The audio is processed on the watch and is never recorded or sent.
Do I need the iPhone app?
No. The measurement runs on the Apple Watch. The iPhone app only carries the watch app and provides help and links.
The temperature is wrong.
Switch off “Apple Weather” in Settings and enter the temperature manually.
